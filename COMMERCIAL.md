## Why dual-licensing

The AGPL protects the project: anyone can benefit from it and improve it, but no
one can quietly take it closed or profit from it without contributing back — and
infringement of the AGPL is enforceable as copyright infringement. The
commercial license is the sanctioned path for organisations that need
proprietary terms, and it is how the project is funded.
